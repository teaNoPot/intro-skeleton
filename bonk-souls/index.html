You don't pick a character. Mildhollow picks for you. There are only two heroes, Pip the turnip and Radia the radish. Everyone else is a monster, a cactus, a rock, a pot, a tree, an apple, the wind, the boss frog, or the mountain. The world and both heroes are shared and saved, so the hero you leave behind is the hero somebody else picks up.
